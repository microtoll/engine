Microtoll invites

Inviting a known person directly: no link, nothing to forward. Share one note by link, press "remember people" on both sides, and from then on invite each other through mailboxes only the two of you can compute. The server sees labels and ciphertext; it cannot say who invited whom. Read invites.js for the whole model.
